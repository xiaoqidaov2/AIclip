# Vision Inspection Skill

Use this skill to inspect images or videos and extract structured visual observations before any editing.

Use it for:
- scene and subject summary
- OCR text extraction
- face/object/layout detection
- content understanding for later editing decisions

Do not use this skill for:
- trimming or timeline edits
- transcription
- render/export
- CapCut draft packaging

Tools:
- `vision_analyze_media`
